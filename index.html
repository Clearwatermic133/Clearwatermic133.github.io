Services — Work With Us

Everything below is done by the same two producers behind this site. No outsourcing, no assembly line. Tell us what you are making, and we will write back personally with a price and a timeline before any money changes hands.

Mixing

Send your raw tracks and get back a balanced, release-ready mix — vocals up front, low end under control, everything in its place.

Mastering

The final polish. Loudness, tone, and clarity set for streaming, so your song holds up next to anything else in the playlist.

Full Audio Production

Have a voice memo or a guitar demo? We build the whole record around it — arrangement, instruments, and a finished mix.
